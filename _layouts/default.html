Research

My research lies at the intersection of AI, security, and programming languages. I study how AI reshapes programming and how we can safely benefit from its capabilities.

  • Security and safety are my primary motivation. Through the lens of cybersecurity, my work has revealed emerging risks posed by increasingly capable AI agents and has been adopted across the frontier AI industry (CyberGym, ExploitGym, SVEN).
  • Guarantees by construction are my response. AI can absorb much of the effort traditionally required to apply techniques in programming languages and formal methods, making formal guarantees practical at unprecedented scale. My work uses type systems, compiler feedback, and formal verification to make AI-generated code correct and secure by construction.
